About Shop4U

Shop4U is a friendly online store built to make everyday shopping simple. We bring together useful electronics, comfortable fashion, home essentials and handy accessories at honest prices.

Curated range

Every product is picked for quality and value, with clear descriptions and real stock counts.

Pay on delivery

No card needed. Order online and pay in cash when your parcel arrives.

Safe accounts

Passwords are securely hashed and your order history stays private to you.

Our story

Shop4U started as a college project with one goal: show how a modern, honest store can be built from the ground up. Today it serves as a complete demo of browsing, carts, accounts, checkout and order management.
